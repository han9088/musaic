Local music and music folders

Local Music uses media the operating system lets Musaic access. Music Folders uses the directories you choose and their accessible subfolders. Add and play files you have the right to use. The app depends on system permissions, file locations, formats, and decoding support. A track may be visible but unplayable if a cloud file is not downloaded, a drive is disconnected, the file has moved, the format is unsupported, or digital rights protection applies.

Adding a folder creates an index for browsing and playback in Musaic. Removing the folder updates that index. You and your storage provider continue to manage the original files. The index and artwork cache are not backups of your music.

You may use your existing music files as the law permits. Buying a file, obtaining access, and obtaining a license for public performance or commercial use can involve different rights.
