使用音乐源前，先了解这三件事Three things to know before choosing a music source

  • 连接 Apple Music 需要你授权。搜索和播放请求通过 Apple 的服务处理。
  • 本地曲库和音乐文件夹在设备上扫描、整理和播放。Musaic 不会把本地曲库或原始音频上传到自己的服务器。
  • 切换音乐源、移除文件夹和撤销系统权限各有不同作用。下文说明怎样停止访问及清理已保存的数据。
  • Connecting Apple Music requires your permission. Search and playback requests go through Apple’s services.
  • Musaic scans, organizes, and plays local music on your device. It does not upload your local library or original audio to its own servers.
  • Switching sources, removing a folder, and revoking system permissions do different things. This policy explains how to stop access and clear stored data.

当你使用 Apple Music、从应用商店购买或打开外部链接时，相关服务对其自行处理的信息适用各自的隐私规则。

This policy explains how deskbtm, the publisher of Musaic, handles personal information. It covers the mobile, desktop, and web versions of Musaic. Features and permissions vary by platform and version. In this policy, “we” means deskbtm, the publisher of Musaic.

Apple Music, app stores, and external sites apply their own privacy policies to the information they process for their services.

音乐源和曲库数据Music sources and library data

本地音乐（设备的系统曲库）

当你在支持的平台主动选择「本地音乐」时，Musaic 会通过系统媒体接口检查或请求音乐访问权限。Android 通过系统媒体库访问获准的音频，iOS 通过系统音乐资料库读取获准的条目。权限范围由系统决定，和通过文件选择器选定一个文件夹的授权范围不同。

授权后，Musaic 会按需读取曲名、专辑、艺人、曲序、时长、媒体标识及播放地址，以及可用的专辑封面，用于分组、显示和播放。播放时，Musaic 通过设备的媒体播放器打开被选中的音频。系统曲库扫描结果作为当前会话的内存快照使用，不写入音乐文件夹的数据库。能否播放取决于系统提供的文件、权限和版权保护状态。

你可以通过系统设置撤销该权限，使后续读取停止。切换到其他音乐源只会改变 App 当前使用的来源。系统权限需要在设备上单独管理。

音乐文件夹

你通过系统文件选择器添加文件夹后，Musaic 会读取该文件夹及其可访问的子目录，以识别音频和相关封面，建立专辑列表，并播放你选择的文件。对于音频，Musaic 会读取文件名、路径或系统文件标识、文件大小、修改时间，以及可用的曲名、艺人、专辑、曲序、时长等标签。封面可能取自音频内嵌图片或同目录的封面文件。

为让你重新打开 App 时继续浏览，Musaic 会在设备上保留选定文件夹的访问凭据、曲库索引和封面缓存。扫描和播放使用文件所在位置。若文件由 iCloud Drive 或其他文件提供方管理，打开文件时该提供方可能联网取得内容，并按其自己的规则处理请求。

保留文件夹授权后，Musaic 可以在恢复该来源或刷新索引时重新读取选定目录。扫描会使用文件位置、修改时间等信息判断条目是否变化。只需授权你想纳入曲库的目录。

Apple Music

授权与读取范围。选择 Apple Music 音乐源后，你可以通过 Apple 的授权流程将其连接到 Musaic。授权后，Musaic 通过 MusicKit 读取用于显示和播放的信息，包括 Apple Music 商店地区、播放权限、已加入资料库的专辑和可访问的曲目，以及名称、艺人、时长、内容标识、静态封面和 Apple 提供的动态封面。Apple 负责处理你的 Apple 账户密码和验证码。

搜索、封面和播放请求。你浏览、搜索或播放时，Musaic 会通过 Apple 的服务发出相应请求。请求可能包含搜索词、商店地区、曲目或专辑标识，以及用于确认授权的 Apple 访问凭据。Apple 提供曲库、封面和音频串流，并按自己的规则处理收到的搜索、播放与账号服务信息。Apple Music 与隐私

撤回授权。你可以在系统或 Apple 账户的授权设置中撤回 Musaic 的访问权限。依赖该授权的资料库和播放功能将无法使用。Apple Music 资料库与播放记录由 Apple 管理，可在 Apple Music 或 Apple 账户中查看相关选项。

Local music (your device’s system library)

When you choose Local Music on a supported platform, Musaic checks or requests music permission through the operating system. Android grants access to eligible audio through its system media library, and iOS grants access to eligible items through its system music library. The operating system determines the scope of this permission, which differs from access to a folder you choose in the file picker.

Once authorized, Musaic reads track titles, albums, artists, track numbers, durations, media identifiers, and playback addresses as needed, along with available artwork. It uses these to group, show, and play your music. To play a track, Musaic opens the selected audio through the device media player. The system library scan is kept in memory for the current session and is not written to the music folder database. Playback depends on file availability, permissions, and content protection.

You can revoke this permission in system settings to stop further access. Switching to a different source changes the source in use. You manage system permissions separately on your device.

Music folders

When you add a folder with the system file picker, Musaic reads it and its accessible subfolders to find audio and artwork, build your album list, and play the files you choose. It reads file names, paths or system file identifiers, sizes, modification times, and available tags such as track title, artist, album, track number, and duration. Artwork may come from embedded images or cover files in the same folder.

Musaic keeps folder access credentials, a library index, and an artwork cache on your device so you can resume browsing when you reopen the app. It scans and plays audio from where the files are stored. If iCloud Drive or another file provider manages a file, that provider may retrieve the file over the internet and handle the request under its own policy.

While folder access is active, Musaic can read the selected folder again when it restores that source or refreshes the index. It uses information such as file locations and modification times to detect changes. Choose only folders you want included in your library.

Apple Music

Authorization and access. When you choose Apple Music as your music source, you can connect it to Musaic through Apple’s authorization flow. Once authorized, Musaic uses MusicKit to read information needed to show and play music. This includes your Apple Music storefront, playback eligibility, albums added to your library and accessible tracks, and details such as titles, artists, durations, content identifiers, still artwork, and animated artwork provided by Apple. Apple handles your Apple Account password and verification codes.

Search, artwork, and playback requests. When you browse, search, or play music, Musaic makes the corresponding requests through Apple’s services. Requests may include search terms, your storefront, track or album identifiers, and Apple credentials used to verify authorization. Apple supplies the library, artwork, and audio stream and handles the search, playback, and account service information it receives under its own policy. Apple Music & Privacy

Revoking access. You can revoke Musaic’s access in your system or Apple Account authorization settings. Library and playback features that depend on that authorization will become unavailable. Apple manages your Apple Music library and listening history. Look for the relevant controls in Apple Music or your Apple Account.

封面、相机与故障反馈Artwork, camera, and diagnostics

自定义封面。当你在系统图片选择器中选取图片时，Musaic 读取你选中的图片并在设备上保存用于替换的封面。你可以在 App 中还原原封面。通过相册删除原图和在 Musaic 中还原封面是各自独立的操作。

相机与相册。Cover Flow 的相机背景在你启用后使用相机。选择保存画面时，Musaic 在设备上将相机图像和可见的界面元素合成并写入相册。授权相机只用于开启该功能。已保存的照片由你在相册中管理。你通过系统分享或其他 App 发送照片时，你选定的接收方会收到相应图像。

本地诊断。Musaic 保存最近最多 300 条应用事件，可能包括事件时间、类别、扫描计数、错误类型和相关代码位置。生成报告时会加入设备型号、系统版本和 App 版本。报告会在你主动发送或分享时交给你选择的接收方。你也可以只导出文件并在发送前查看内容。

支持和偏好。你联系我们时，我们会处理你的邮箱地址、往来邮件和你附上的截图、报告或订单参考信息，用于回复、排查或处理请求。语言、外观和音乐源选择由 App 在设备上保存，用于恢复你的设置。

Custom artwork. When you choose an image in the system picker, Musaic reads the selected image and saves the replacement cover on your device. You can restore the original cover in the app. Deleting the source image from your photo library and restoring a cover in Musaic are separate actions.

Camera and photo library. The Cover Flow camera background uses the camera when you turn the feature on. When you choose to save a scene, Musaic combines the camera image and visible interface elements on your device and writes the result to your photo library. Camera permission enables this feature. You manage saved photos in your photo library. If you send a photo through system sharing or another app, it goes to the recipient you choose.

Local diagnostics. Musaic stores up to 300 recent app events. These may include timestamps, event categories, scan counts, error types, and relevant code locations. When you generate a report, it includes your device model, operating system version, and app version. A report goes to the recipient you choose when you send or share it. You can export a copy and review it before sending.

Support and preferences. When you contact us, we process your email address, our correspondence, and any screenshots, reports, or order references you include to respond, investigate, or handle your request. The app saves language, appearance, and music source preferences on your device to restore your settings.

保存期限与清理Retention and deletion

  • 撤销系统曲库权限。在系统设置中撤销 Musaic 的音乐权限后，App 将无法继续读取需要该权限的系统曲库数据。系统曲库的扫描结果作为当前会话的内存数据使用。
  • 移除音乐文件夹。在「设置 → 音乐源 → 音乐文件夹」中移除文件夹，会移除关联的授权记录以及其他已添加文件夹不再引用的索引。涉及重叠文件夹时，仍获准访问的目录可以保留同一文件的索引。你在文件管理器或存储服务中管理原音乐文件。
  • 清理 App 数据。切换音乐源和撤回访问权限不会自动清理已有索引与缓存。已保存的封面、索引、偏好和滚动诊断记录由设备管理，你可以通过系统清除 App 数据或删除 App，具体取决于平台和备份设置。网页数据可在浏览器中清除。
  • 处理独立副本。你在相册中保存的照片、导出的报告、邮件附件和设备备份可能在清理 App 后继续保存。请在对应位置管理这些副本。撤销 Musaic 对 Apple Music 的授权会停止受授权的访问。你的 Apple Music 资料库和 Apple 保留的播放记录可在 Apple 的服务里管理。
  • 我们收到的记录。我们只在回复支持请求、核实交易、处理版权或隐私申诉及解决争议所需的期限内保留相关资料，或者依法定期限保存。达到保存目的或期限后，依法删除或匿名化。法律要求继续保留的信息依法律规定限制用途。商店、Apple Music 和邮件服务管理各自保存的信息。
  • Revoke system library access. Revoking Musaic’s music permission in system settings stops access to system library information that requires that permission. System library scan results are used in memory for the current session.
  • Remove a music folder. Remove a folder in Settings → Music source → Music folders to remove its authorization record and index entries that other added folders no longer reference. An overlapping folder that remains authorized can keep a reference to the same file. Manage the original files with your file manager or storage provider.
  • Clear app data. Switching music sources or withdrawing access does not automatically clear existing indexes or caches. Stored covers, indexes, preferences, and recent diagnostics are kept on the device. Use system settings to clear app data or delete the app, depending on your platform and backup settings. Clear web data in your browser.
  • Manage independent copies. Photos saved to your photo library, exported reports, mail attachments, and device backups can remain after clearing app data. Manage these copies where you saved them. Revoking Musaic’s Apple Music authorization stops access that depends on it. Manage your Apple Music library and the listening history Apple retains in Apple’s services.
  • Records we receive. We keep information for the period needed to respond to support, verify transactions, handle copyright or privacy complaints, and resolve disputes, or for a period required by law. We delete or anonymize it in accordance with applicable law after the purpose or retention period ends. When retention is required by law, we restrict use as the law requires. Stores, Apple Music, and email services manage the information they retain.

We use safeguards appropriate to the type of information and the risks to protect the information we process from unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration. We restrict access to support, transaction, and complaint records and use them to the extent needed to handle the request. If a personal information security incident occurs, we take corrective action and notify affected users and authorities where applicable law requires it.

When emailing us, start with the minimum information needed to identify the issue. Keep passwords, verification codes, and payment credentials private. You can redact unrelated details from screenshots or receipts before sending them.
